ABOUT

From student profile to actionable guidance.

Placify AI combines a trained Decision Tree model with an interactive Streamlit interface. Authenticated users can make predictions, review their own prediction history and receive practical improvement guidance.

RESPONSIBLE USE

Designed for education, not automated decisions.

Placify AI does not guarantee employment or placement. It must not be used as the sole basis for hiring, admissions, scholarships, employment, or other high-impact decisions. A real institutional deployment would require authorized data, privacy safeguards, representative sampling, fairness evaluation and independent validation.
